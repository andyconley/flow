# Sustainability Standard

This standard defines how software efficiency and environmental impact are considered in engineering decisions.

## Core principle

Efficiency improvements often reduce both cost and carbon impact.

## Practical defaults

- eliminate idle waste
- prefer efficient compute choices when tradeoffs are reasonable
- use scaling and caching deliberately
- consider region and workload timing when those choices materially affect energy or carbon profile

## Measurement

When sustainability matters to the project, define a measurable unit and track it alongside cost and performance rather than treating it as a vague aspiration.

## Relevant standards and tools

Principles:

- software carbon intensity thinking
- efficiency as a design concern

Useful references:

- SCI
- carbon-aware scheduling
- cloud carbon footprint tooling
