# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

This project works best when people are direct, specific, and respectful.

- Assume good intent, but make impact clear when something causes harm.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, the behavior, or the tradeoff.
- Avoid harassment, threats, personal attacks, slurs, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect boundaries when someone asks to end a topic or interaction.
- Do not publish private information without explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Do not use public issues for private, sensitive, or personally identifying reports.

Include enough context to understand what happened: links, screenshots, dates, usernames, and any prior attempts to resolve the issue.

## Response

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, block users, or limit participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

Reports will be handled with as much privacy as the situation allows. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
