You are advising a product team with one delivery team. The team must complete
three independent options one at a time. Work on an option is uninterrupted,
and its benefit starts only when it is complete.

- Enterprise dashboard: executives call it the most important strategic item.
  It is worth $1,000,000 whenever it ships during the next 12 weeks. Delivery
  takes 6 weeks, and a week's delay does not reduce that value in this horizon.
- Renewal compliance: it prevents a one-time $240,000 loss only if complete by
  the end of week 4. Starting one week late means the deadline is missed and
  the full loss occurs. Delivery takes 4 weeks.
- Activation onboarding: its expected benefit falls by $25,000 for every week
  completion is delayed. Delivery takes 1 week.

Recommend an exact delivery sequence. Make the decision basis reproducible
from the supplied values, and explain why the executives' importance ranking
may or may not determine the sequence. Do not ask for more information.
