# Definition: narrow the expertise expansion to evidenced roles

- Work item: `role-method-differentiation-repair-3`
- Definition lead: solution-architect
- Opening role: solution-architect
- Status: Approved
- Approver: Andy Conley
- Approved: 2026-09-09 (America/New_York)

## Problem or opportunity

- **What:** The working-tree expertise expansion combines three roles with
  demonstrated treatment-over-control behavior and two roles that failed that
  gate twice. Keeping all five coupled blocks a truthful release. Shipping the
  two failed methods under the same improvement claim would overstate the
  evidence and add active prompt material without demonstrated marginal value.
- **Who:** Flow maintainers and users who rely on generated architect,
  lead-developer, test-engineer, product-manager, and quality-reviewer agents.
- **Why now:** Architect, test-engineer, and lead-developer already have retained
  passing evidence. Keeping them coupled to two failed gates delays an
  evidence-correct release, while no deadline or measured PM/QR workflow harm
  makes a third experiment urgent. Narrowing is reversible and preserves the
  option to reopen either deferred role with a separate evidence envelope.

## Desired outcome

Release the source-backed architect, test-engineer, and lead-developer additions
as a three-role expansion. Product-manager and quality-reviewer remain available
as base roles: their next generated sync uses the unchanged role contracts and
contains no composed expertise section, and refreshed user installs reconcile
stale generated copies. Preserve both failed PM/QR evidence generations as
durable research, and defer any new candidate screen to a separately approved
work item.

## Requirements

1. Define the release as an expansion of exactly three newly composed roles:
   architect, lead-developer, and test-engineer. The pre-existing composed roles
   business-analyst, SRE, and support-lead remain unchanged.
2. Keep the architect, lead-developer, and test-engineer role instructions,
   source-backed corpus entries, competency edges, composition declarations,
   and passing behavioral evidence that support the narrowed claim.
3. Remove the repair-2 product-manager and quality-reviewer methods from active
   role instructions, shipped corpora, competency edges, composition
   declarations, rendered agent bodies, and current-cohort documentation.
   Keep both roles available through their exact frozen pre-repair base bodies,
   and make the normal sync path remove stale generated expertise sections.
4. Preserve the original and repair-2 PM/QR designs, raw responses, manifests,
   scores, reviews, and source-verification records as failed historical
   evidence. The protected set is the per-file inventory captured before any
   repair-3 production mutation. Do not delete, rescore, relabel, reformat, or
   imply that either role passed.
5. State separately what the release proves: provenance, valid composition, and
   behavioral differentiation for the three added roles. Do not report a
   five-role behavioral or composition release.
6. Use the captured before-change hashes for the three retained role
   contracts/corpora and both failed evidence trees, then compare them after the
   narrowing change.
   Any unexpected delta blocks acceptance until dispositioned.
7. Update deterministic tests, architecture/file-layout documentation, and
   release-facing language to the exact six-role composed cohort and three-role
   expansion. Put the new current-release evidence summary outside all protected
   historical evidence roots; historical READMEs remain byte-identical.
8. Keep PM/QR candidate exploration outside this release. The leading future
   candidates are resolvable success metrics and evidence-strength-aware review;
   they require a named workflow failure or adoption need, verified source
   locators, explicit engineer approval of a fixed exploration budget, and a
   bounded control-gap screen before a new acceptance envelope is approved.
9. For this release only, active composed additions require demonstrated
   behavioral differentiation. A general policy allowing provenance-only active
   prompt content remains a valid but deferred alternative; it is not inferred
   from this run-local removal decision.

## Success criteria

Repair-3 succeeds as an evidence-correct technical release when the conditions
below resolve at formal review. It makes no adoption, support-load, business-
impact, or global-agent-improvement claim.

- The configuration and generated-body inventory reports exactly six composed
  roles, with exactly architect, lead-developer, and test-engineer added by this
  expansion. Any seventh/eighth role or missing retained role fails the release.
- All three released methods have a source/locator, valid forward and reverse
  competency joins, deterministic rendering in both adapters, and retained
  passing behavioral evidence. The threshold is `3/3`; any missing or failed
  role record blocks acceptance.
- Active product-manager and quality-reviewer bodies contain zero repair-2
  instructions and zero composed expertise entries. Their evidence result
  remains explicitly failed in both evidence summaries and the implementation
  handoff. Any active method or passing implication fails the release.
- The captured before/after digests match for retained role evidence, both
  failed PM/QR evidence trees, and the complete protected per-file inventory.
  The measurement source is the versioned hash receipt; an absent baseline or
  unexplained mismatch fails the preservation claim.
- Repository tests, whitespace, generated Claude and Codex checks, static
  runtime smoke, and doctor all pass against the final tree. At least one newly
  composed role is also loaded through each live client after the develop
  install is refreshed. Both live observations are acceptance requirements;
  if either client is unavailable or the expected method/model/effort cannot be
  observed, acceptance remains blocked.

## Non-goals

- A third PM/QR behavioral run, candidate implementation, or source adoption.
- A two-axis evidence-status schema or general policy that allows
  provenance-only entries to ship as active prompt content. It is deferred
  because its value and runtime cost are unmeasured, not rejected as an invalid
  architecture.
- Retrieval, archive indexing, ranker behavior, model routing, overlay merging,
  corpus storage, renderer redesign, or changes to other agents.
- Revalidating every prior role across new models or claiming global agent
  improvement from bounded fixtures.
- Merging, publishing, or releasing before implementation review accepts the
  narrowed result.

## Constraints

- This definition supersedes repair-2's approved all-five release relationship
  only after explicit engineer approval.
- Approval supersedes only that release relationship. It does not weaken or
  supersede repair-2's scores, stopping rule, failure labels, or evidence.
- Repair-2 is immutable. Any later PM/QR method, prompt, fixture, rubric, model
  setting, or stopping-rule change starts a new versioned envelope.
- A source citation, deterministic render, longer answer, or safe counter alone

- Production removal must not erase historical evidence or change the three

- Missing archive retrieval remains visible and supplies no authority claim.

## Assumptions

- The retained evidence correctly identifies architect, test-engineer, and
  lead-developer as the three passing additions. Confidence: high from the
  frozen results and formal review; implementation must reverify hashes.
- Removing active PM/QR composition is local and reversible because their
  methods are uncommitted and their evidence is stored separately. Confidence:
  high from the current diff and architecture inspection.
- A three-role expansion has standalone value even though no usage or support
  metric exists yet. Confidence: medium; the release claim is technical and
  evidence-scoped rather than an adoption claim.

## Approved decisions

1. Supersede the all-five release relationship with the three-role expansion,
   remove the PM/QR methods from active composition, and defer the
   provenance-only admission policy.
2. Leave the bounded PM/QR candidate screen deferred until a concrete workflow
   failure or adoption need satisfies the trigger in requirement 8.

## Research implications

- Two failed comparisons with substantive control overlap -> stop treating
  wording refinement as the default repair.
- Three retained passing roles plus low-cost reversibility -> release the
  evidence-supported subset first.
- Provenance, composition, and behavior are distinct claims -> state only the
  dimensions proven for shipped roles, while avoiding a new status architecture
  in this bounded release.
- Stronger future candidates remain untested -> retain them as research
  directions, not current requirements or active prompt content.
- Missing archive index -> keep the retrieval miss visible and do not infer that
  manually inspected decisions were retrieved.
