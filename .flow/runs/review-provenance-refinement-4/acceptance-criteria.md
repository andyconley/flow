# Acceptance Criteria

1. All AC1-AC10 from `review-provenance-refinement-2` remain binding.
2. `approve-definition` validates the canonical Shaper intent schema before recording approval or digests; invalid intent leaves state/history unchanged.
3. Planning guidance and generated artifacts distinguish direct/manual orchestration from a chartered Delivery Lead job.
4. Approval or dispatch rejects a Delivery Lead plan lacking `magentic-manager`, provider/model bindings, a bounded editable producer, independent read-only verifier, linked `job-charter.json`, safe targeted test, or isolated worker-worktree contract.
5. The executable roster remains within the sealed Delivery Charter and cannot widen paths, tools, providers, roles, cardinality, or producer/verifier separation.
6. The successor dispatches through `execute-chartered-job` in a separate clean pinned worktree; no manual implementation substitute is used.
7. Focused tests reproduce both predecessor failures and prove rejection at the earliest gate.
8. The completed amendment is applied to `maf-runtime-readiness-2`, followed by fresh quality, test, security, and SRE review, acceptance validation, acceptance, and archive when green.
9. A Shaper or Delivery envelope above the protocol-v8 runtime or Magentic ceilings is rejected before implementation; the executable successor uses a 600-second runtime allowance.
