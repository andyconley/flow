# Product Review

- The smallest valuable outcome is an enforceable verifier signal rather than more verifier prose or another Ollama feasibility exercise.
- Require one bounded structured verdict, record its evaluation in the receipt, and default `max_verifier_calls` to one for this slice.
- Do not claim the schema improves model judgment. It makes weak or malformed judgment visible and safely terminal.
- Defer retries, repair loops, quorum, semantic scoring, provider routing changes, and nested subagents.
