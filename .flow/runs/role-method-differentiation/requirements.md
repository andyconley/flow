# Definition: role-method differentiation

## Problem

The first medium-confidence expertise expansion did not create a measurable
behavioral advantage for product-manager or quality-reviewer. A second pass
showed that broad additions still overlapped the unchanged role bodies. The
lead-developer short/full-plan method did produce a distinct result and must
remain unchanged.

## Approved repair

1. Replace the redundant product-manager priority entry with one source-traced
   method for time-sensitive sequencing. It must compare value decay or cost of
   delay with delivery duration rather than rank by importance alone.
2. Replace the redundant quality-reviewer acceptance entry with one
   source-traced method for calibrating Critical findings. Before filing a
   Critical, the reviewer must test a plausible explanation under which the
   artifact is correct and cite the evidence that rules it out. If the
   explanation cannot be ruled out, the item remains a question or lower-
   severity finding.
3. Remove superseded entries and competency edges while retaining their prior
   evidence as a historical failed attempt.
4. Change only the minimum role-body wording needed to make each replacement
   method executable. Do not retain the unselected product-manager
   reversibility/metric additions or quality-reviewer omission/evidence-label
   additions in this repair.
5. Preserve the passing lead-developer short/full-plan change and its evidence
   without further behavior changes.
6. Freeze exact treatment/control bodies, prompts, client settings, rubrics,
   and stopping rules before new runs. Treatment passes only when it satisfies
   every distinctive rubric item that the unchanged-body control misses;
   ties and counter-case over-application fail.
7. Keep retrieval, model routing, expertise architecture, and the other role
   methods outside this repair.

## Release relationship

This repair closes the two remaining failed role gates in the earlier
five-role expansion. Architect and test-engineer retain their passing evidence;
lead-developer retains its passing differentiation evidence. The combined
five-role release is eligible for review only if both replacement methods pass.
