# Acceptance criteria: role-method differentiation repair

1. Product-manager has one replacement entry with a verified Reinertsen
   edition and locator. The superseded outcome/evidence entry and its
   competency edge are absent.
2. In the frozen primary case, the product-manager treatment states each
   option's time-value shape, compares cost of delay with delivery duration,
   and sequences by value lost through delay. The unchanged-body control misses
   every one of those distinctive items. A flat-value counter-case does not
   invent urgency or decay.
3. Quality-reviewer has one replacement entry with a verified Heuer source and
   Chapter 8 locator. The superseded observable-proof and omission/evidence-
   strength entries and their competency edges are absent.
4. In the frozen primary case, the quality-reviewer treatment states a
   plausible explanation under which the artifact is correct, cites the exact
   evidence that rules it out, and only then files a Critical. The unchanged-
   body control misses every distinctive item. In the counter-case, where the
   explanation remains unresolved, treatment asks a question or lowers
   severity instead of filing a Critical.
5. Lead-developer's passing short/full-plan role contract, corpus entry, and
   prior behavioral evidence remain unchanged.
6. Deterministic tests prove the replacement entry ids, competency joins,
   minimum role-body contracts, composed-role manifest, and shared rendering
   path. A mutation check proves the new executable-boundary assertion fails
   when one selected instruction is removed.
7. Focused and full tests, whitespace checks, both generated-adapter checks,
   static runtime smoke, and doctor pass against the final working tree.
8. Original failed transcripts remain intact and are marked superseded by a
   separately frozen repair evidence set. The combined five-role result cites
   architect and test-engineer's prior passes, lead-developer's differentiation
   pass, and new product-manager and quality-reviewer passes.
