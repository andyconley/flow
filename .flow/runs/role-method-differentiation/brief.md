# Definition brief: role-method differentiation

## Problem

The current product-manager, quality-reviewer, and lead-developer role bodies
already cover the first expansion's generic guidance. The evidence showed no
measurable treatment advantage for two roles and over-planning for the third.

## Candidate scope

- Product manager: cost-of-delay shape, reversibility as decision rigor, and
  resolvable success/kill conditions.
- Quality reviewer: spec-derived omission checks, alternative explanation for
  critical findings, and evidence-strength labels in the verification story.
- Lead developer: risk-proportionate plan depth and an explicit short-form plan
  for atomic, reversible, well-tested changes.

## Guardrails

Do not copy the proposals into agents until source tracing, role-boundary
review, and discriminating treatment/control fixtures show a role-owned change.
The lead-developer short form is a role-body/output-format change, not a corpus
entry alone. The blocked five-role expansion remains separate and unmerged.
